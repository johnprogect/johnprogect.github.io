Mountain equipment

It is not the mountain we conquer, but ourselves.

The summit is what drives us, but the climb itself is what matters.

Great things are done when men and mountains meet.

scheme of typical mountain up to 1000 meters

Core gear:

  • Trail shoes / light hiking boots
  • Comfortable backpack (10–20L)
  • Water (1–2L)
  • Basic food/snacks
  • Light rain jacket
  • Phone + offline maps (e.g. AllTrails)
  • Optional:
    • Trekking poles
    • Hat / sunscreen

👉 This is essentially hiking, not mountaineering.

scheme of typical mountain up to 2000 meters

Environment: steeper terrain, weather changes fast Add to previous:

  • Proper hiking boots (ankle support)
  • Layering system: base layer (moisture-wicking) fleece or light insulation shell jacket (wind/rain)
  • Headlamp
  • First aid kit
  • Emergency blanket

👉 Risk starts to come from weather shifts and fatigue, not terrain yet.

scheme of typical mountain up to 3000 meters

Critical additions:

  • Mountaineering boots (stiffer soles)
  • Gaiters (snow protection): Crampons Ice axe shell jacket (wind/rain)
  • Headlamp Helmet (now strongly) recomendid
  • Altitude meds awareness (risk of acute mountain sickness)
  • Emergency bivy shelter: base layer (moisture-wicking) fleece or light insulation shell jacket (wind/rain)

👉 This is essentially hiking, not mountaineering.
